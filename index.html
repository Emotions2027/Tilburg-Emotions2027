Welcome to Emotions 2027

Join us from October 6 - 8, 2027 for Emotions 2027. This international conference at Tilburg University (The Netherlands) is dedicated to advancing the scientific study of emotions.

The Emotions conference is a long-standing international meeting that has brought together researchers from diverse disciplines who share an interest in the scientific study of emotions. Building on this tradition, Emotions 2027 will provide a platform for presenting new empirical findings, discussing theoretical developments, and showcasing methodological innovations.

We welcome contributions from a wide range of disciplines, including psychiatry, psychosomatics, health and clinical psychology, developmental psychology, cultural and social psychology, neuroscience, economics, behavioral medicine, communication science, and related fields. By bringing together diverse perspectives, the conference aims to foster interdisciplinary dialogue and new collaborations.
