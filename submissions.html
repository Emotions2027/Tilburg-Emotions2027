The Call for Abstracts will open soon.

Researchers from all areas of emotion and affective science are invited to submit their work for presentation at Emotions 2027. Contributions may be submitted for oral presentations, symposia, poster sessions, and other conference formats.

We welcome empirical, theoretical, methodological, and applied research from a broad range of disciplines. Detailed submission guidelines, deadlines, and review procedures will be announced soon.

We look forward to receiving your contributions and showcasing the latest developments in emotion research.
