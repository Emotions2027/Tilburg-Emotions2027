Keynote speakers

Emotions 2027 will welcome four keynote speakers from across contemporary emotion science.

Raffael Kalisch

University Medical Center Mainz · Leibniz Institute for Resilience Research

Raffael Kalisch studies the cognitive and neurobiological mechanisms that help people maintain mental health in the face of stress and adversity, with a particular focus on resilience.

Institutional profile →

Disa Sauter

University of Oxford

Disa Sauter's research examines emotion and behaviour, including nonverbal vocal expressions, cross-cultural variation, positive emotion, and the role of emotion in pro-environmental behaviour.

Institutional profile →

Renee Thompson

Washington University in St. Louis

Renee Thompson investigates how emotions and emotion regulation unfold over time in daily life, and how these dynamics contribute to depression and other forms of psychopathology.

Institutional profile →

Aidan Wright

University of Michigan

Aidan Wright studies personality and psychopathology, social and interpersonal processes, and person-specific dynamics using quantitative and ambulatory assessment methods.

Institutional profile →
